### The 2 e2e failures

Both in `e2e/videos.spec.ts`, both asserting a Facebook video iframe renders on `/videos`
and the homepage reel. This is a pre-existing, already-documented issue, not a regression
from today's batch: the one video URL in `config/videos.ts` is a `/share/v/...` shortlink
that Facebook's embed player rejects (`todo/my-work/for_you.md`, `todo/agent-work/README.md`
"Known follow-ups"). It's gated on the human owner getting a canonical permalink from the
original post. Every other suite (calculator, tracker, rates, tools, tracker adversarial
input, site nav) passed in full.
