# Creator pitches and community script (drafts, 2026-11-01)

Drafts for the owner to send. Nothing here has been sent, posted or commented. Channels are listed in `docs/gtm/creators.csv` and communities in `docs/gtm/groups.csv`, all checked on 2026-11-01. Each row has its own `?src=` link.

How to send: use the contact in `creators.csv` (email first, then the Facebook page). Use one message per creator, from your own account, and nothing in the same week as your Nepal trip (3-9 Nov). Log replies in the `status` column.

Do not contact:
- HR or payroll firms, or anyone selling to employers. TakaTalks is frozen for that market until you decide who owns it. Before you write to `rmconsult`, check that RM CONSULTANCY is not an HR or payroll firm.
- Stock "tips" groups. BSEC named "BD Stock Exchange (DSE & CSE)", "Public Business Club", "DSE Investors Club" and "Share Market - DSE Investors Club" in manipulation and scam warnings (bdnews24, 9 Sep 2025; businessinsiderbd.com).

What every pitch can promise, and nothing more: the calculator is free and carries no ads, and the numbers someone types stay in their browser (`/privacy`, `CODING_STANDARDS.md` "Privacy"). Its tax rules come from the Finance Act 2026 as summarised by KPMG and NBR (`web/src/config/tax-rules-2025-26.ts`).
