# Video Concept: If your salary is X, which bike should you buy?

## The Core Rule
When calculating budgets, the 50/30/20 rule is a great baseline:
- **50% Needs**
- **30% Wants**
- **20% Savings/Investing**

For this video, we dedicate a maximum of **15% (half of the 'Wants' category)** specifically to cover the **monthly cost of owning a bike**. 
**Monthly Bike Cost = EMI + Fuel + Maintenance**

---

## 🚲 Tier 1: Salary = 30,000 BDT
* **Allocated Budget (15%):** 4,500 BDT / month
* **Running Cost (Fuel/Maintenance):** ~1,500 BDT
* **Left for EMI:** ~3,000 BDT

**The Verdict:** 
Taking an EMI on this salary is a financial trap. It will eat into your emergency savings. 
**Recommendation:** Save up cash for 6-8 months and buy a reliable used entry-level commuter (e.g., TVS XL 100, older Bajaj CT 100) for around 50,000 - 60,000 BDT.

---

## 🏍️ Tier 2: Salary = 60,000 BDT
* **Allocated Budget (15%):** 9,000 BDT / month
* **Running Cost (Fuel/Maintenance):** ~2,500 BDT
* **Left for EMI:** ~6,500 BDT

**The Verdict:** 
Assuming a standard 24-month EMI, you can afford a bike with a total price tag of **1.2 Lakh to 1.5 Lakh BDT**.
**Recommendation:** Go for reliable 110cc-125cc commuters that offer great mileage. 
*Examples:* Honda Dream 110, Bajaj Discover 125, or TVS Raider 125.

---

## 🏁 Tier 3: Salary = 100,000 BDT
* **Allocated Budget (15%):** 15,000 BDT / month
* **Running Cost (Fuel/Maintenance):** ~3,500 BDT
* **Left for EMI:** ~11,500 BDT

**The Verdict:** 
On a 24-month EMI, you can comfortably afford a bike worth **2.5 Lakh to 3 Lakh BDT**.
**Recommendation:** You have room for premium commuters and naked sports bikes. 
*Examples:* Yamaha FZS V3, Honda Hornet 2.0, or Suzuki Gixxer Fi ABS.
