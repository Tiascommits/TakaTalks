# Visual & Production Guide for Content Creators

This guide outlines how to shoot, edit, and produce short-form videos that look professional, clean, and credible while showcasing **TakaTalks** (`takatalks.com`).

---

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Aesthetics

TakaTalks has a distinctive, trustworthy design language rooted in clean editorial typography and a grounded financial color palette.

| Element | Brand Specification | Video Production Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Color** | `green-deep` (`#1A4D2E` / `#163A24`) | Banner backgrounds, main text accents, title cards. |
| **Accent Color** | `gold` (`#D4AF37` / `#C5A059`) | Highlight numbers, rebate savings, important callouts. |
| **Background Color**| `paper` (`#F8F9F5` / `#FAFBF8`) | Clean, non-glaring off-white for screen overlays. |
| **Typography** | Serif (headings) + Monospace (numbers/labels) | Use serif fonts (Playfair Display / Merriweather) for big hooks, and clean mono (JetBrains Mono / Roboto Mono) for financial numbers. |
| **Bengali Fonts** | Hind Siliguri, Noto Sans Bengali | Clean, modern Bengali subtitles with no rendering glitches. |

## 2. Screen Recording Best Practices

The magic of TakaTalks is **instant, reactive calculation with zero page reload and zero signup**. Capture this responsiveness in your screen recordings:

### A. Mobile Screen Recording (Vertical 9:16)
- **Viewport**: Record on an iPhone or Android using Safari/Chrome in clean mode (hide browser bookmarks/toolbars).
- **Smooth Gestures**: Enable touch indicators or tap ripples if possible so viewers see where fingers touch.
- **Micro-Zooms**: When entering an income amount (e.g. `1200000`), punch in 120% zoom directly on the input box, then cut or zoom out smoothly to show the whole line-item breakdown recalculating in real-time.
- **The "Privacy Moment"**: Show the prominent label: *"Calculated on your device. Nothing is sent to our server."* Highlight it with a soft gold oval or glow.

### B. Desktop Screen Recording (Horizontal 16:9 or Split-Screen)
- **Resolution**: Record at 1080p or 4K with browser zoom set to 125% or 150% so numbers and Bengali text are crystal clear on mobile screens.
- **Side-by-Side Setup**: Show the creator on one side (talking head) and the browser screen on the other side, reacting to numbers live.

## 3. Motion Graphics, Overlays & Subtitles

### A. Subtitle Styling
- **Size**: Large, legible subtitles positioned in the middle third (safe zone above platform UI buttons like like/comment/share).
- **Word Highlighting**: Highlight key money terms and numbers in **Gold/Yellow** (`#FFD700`) or **Neon Green** (`#00E676`).
  - *Example*: "Apnar taxable income ashlo **৳8,00,000**" (৳8,00,000 in bright gold).

### B. On-Screen Callout Badges
Use clean, floating pill badges instead of messy generic stickers:
- `[ 🔒 100% On-Device / No Server Storage ]` (Green pill badge)
- `[ ⚡ 0 Seconds Setup / No Account ]` (Gold pill badge)
- `[ 📉 Tax Saved: ৳24,500 ]` (Bright green pill badge)
- `[ 🏛️ Sourced Bank Data / Not Paid ]` (Slate grey pill badge)

## 4. Sound Design & Audio Effects

Sound effects give punch to numerical calculations:
- **Number Input / Typing**: Soft mechanical keyboard click or subtle ticking.
- **Tax Rebate Drop**: Soft "cha-ching" (cash register) or crisp "whoosh" when the tax number drops from ৳45k to ৳20k.
- **Warning / NPL Callout**: Low sub-bass thud or alert chime when discussing bank stress or NPL ratios.
- **Background Music**: Low-volume lo-fi beats or warm acoustic guitar (keep at -22dB to -26dB under vocal track). Never drown out the spoken voice.
