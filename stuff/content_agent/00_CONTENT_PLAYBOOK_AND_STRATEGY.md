# TakaTalks Content Strategy & Playbook

This playbook defines the strategy for creating viral, high-trust, educational short-form content (30s, 60s, up to 3 minutes) that drives active users to **TakaTalks** (`takatalks.com`).

---

## 1. Core Mission & Unique Positioning

### The Problem in Bangladesh
1. **Fear and Distrust of FinTech**: People in Bangladesh are deeply skeptical of financial apps due to past scams (MLM apps, e-commerce Ponzi collapses like Evaly, illegal betting apps). Asking for a phone number or email upfront immediately triggers suspicion.
2. **Banking Sector Uncertainty**: Stressed banking sector, non-performing loans (NPLs >30%), and the modest BDT 2,00,000 deposit insurance limit make people anxious about where their hard-earned money sits.
3. **Tax Confusion & Overpaying**: The Income Tax Act 2023 changed rules, slab exemptions, standard deductions (1/3 of gross or 500k cap), and rebate formulas (3% of taxable income or 10% of investment or 7.5L cap). Most middle-class professionals either overpay taxes or pay accountants for simple math.
4. **Scattered Savings ("Lazy Money")**: People hold scattered FDRs, DPS, and Sanchayapatra accounts across banks, regularly missing maturity dates and leaving money earning 0% or low savings rate.

### TakaTalks Solution & Core Selling Points (The "Cool & Useful" Hooks)
- **Zero-Signup, Trust-First Tax Calculator (`/calculator`)**: 100% client-side calculation in the browser. Zero data sent to servers. No email, no phone number, no password. Open URL -> enter salary -> instant exact tax breakdown.
- **Automated Tax Rebate Optimizer**: Pure mathematical optimization that tells users: *"Invest ৳40,000 more in DPS/Sanchayapatra to save ৳6,000 directly in tax."*
- **Sourced, Unbiased Bank FDR Comparison (`/rates`)**: Side-by-side comparison of real published bank rates with after-tax net return calculated for their tax bracket. No fake sponsored "Top 5 Banks" ranking — pure transparent data.
- **Anonymous Income & Investment Tracker (`/tracker`)**: Session-based, no-friction tracking for salary, freelance income, DPS, and FDRs with 30/60/90-day maturity alerts and reinvestable cash tracking.

## 3. The Winning Short-Form Content Frameworks

### A. The 30-Second "Scroll-Stopper" (TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- **0–3s (Shocking Hook / Contrarian Statement)**: Call out an exact Taka number, a common myth, or a visual showing the site.
- **3–18s (The Instant Demo / Reveal)**: Screen recording of TakaTalks showing how the calculator/tool works immediately with zero friction.
- **18–25s (The Takeaway)**: What they save or avoid (e.g. "Save up to ৳75,000 legally").
- **25–30s (Friction-Free CTA)**: "Link in bio — no email, no signup, runs directly in your browser."

### B. The 60-Second "Problem Solver" (IG Reels, Shorts, Facebook Reels)
- **0–5s (Relatable Pain Point)**: "Every November in Bangladesh, everyone panics about taxes..." or "Banks advertise 11% FDR, but look what actually enters your pocket..."
- **5–20s (Explain the Hidden Mechanism)**: Explain slabs, TDS deduction with/without TIN, or the standard salary exemption.
- **20–45s (Live Tool Solution on Screen)**: Show TakaTalks in action. Slide income, show rebate calculation, show the line-item breakdown.
- **45–55s (Trust / Differentiator)**: Emphasize "Notice how it didn't ask you to login or give your phone number? Everything calculates on your phone."
- **55–60s (CTA)**: "Try your own numbers right now on takatalks.com."

### C. The 2 to 3-Minute "Masterclass / Reality Check" (YouTube, Facebook Video, LinkedIn)
- **0–15s (Context & Stakes)**: The macroeconomic or personal finance reality (e.g. banking stress, inflation vs FDR rates, new national budget changes).
- **15–60s (The Math / The Law Broken Down)**: Clear, non-jargon explanation of the financial rules.
- **60–130s (Step-by-Step Tool Walkthrough)**: Deep dive using TakaTalks features (Rebate Optimizer, FDR After-Tax Comparison, Maturity reminders).
- **130–160s (Strategic Advice / Nuance)**: Why we don't give "best bank" verdicts, how to make independent decisions.
- **160–180s (Outro & Community Trust)**: Free tool, zero data collection, transparent math.

## 4. Voice, Tone & Language Guidelines

- **Language Style**: **Conversational Banglish / Colloquial Dhaka Bangla** mixed with clear English financial terms.
  - *Do say*: "Takar hisab", "Rebate optimizer", "Client-side calculation", "After-tax return", "Slab tax", "Kono signup lagbe na".
  - *Avoid*: Overly formal bookish Bangla ("আয়কর নির্ধারণ প্রক্রিয়া অত্যন্ত জটিল") or dry financial corporate speak. Speak like an experienced tech/finance friend over cha.
- **Honesty Over Hype**: Never say "Get rich quick" or "Guaranteed returns". Highlight *clarity, control, and privacy*.
- **Privacy as a Feature**: Always highlight: *"Calculated on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. No phone number or OTP required."*
