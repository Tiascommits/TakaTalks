Deposit protection, keep this in mind

Bangladesh's Deposit Protection Act (2026) covers up to BDT 2,00,000 per depositor per bank, and only pays out if a bank is formally liquidated. Anything above that, in any single bank, isn't protected. If you're placing a large amount, spreading it across more than one bank reduces this exposure.
